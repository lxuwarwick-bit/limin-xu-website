01 — About 01 — 关于

Truth-seeking beyond conventional boundaries. 在常规边界之外追寻真理。

With a background in bioscience and a doctoral degree from the University of Warwick, Limin Xu is a cross-disciplinary artist whose practice explores the intersections of life, art, and science. She holds an MA in Fine Arts from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and currently serves as Assistant Professor (Research) at the School of Medicine, CUHK Shenzhen. 徐莉敏拥有生物科学背景和华威大学博士学位，是一位跨学科艺术家，致力于探索生命、艺术与科学的交汇。她拥有香港中文大学艺术硕士学位，现任香港中文大学（深圳）医学院助理教授（研究）。

Her artistic practice is driven by a commitment to truth-seeking beyond conventional boundaries, investigating alternative modes of expression through multi-media installation, ink painting, and generative art. Drawing on her research into the mechanisms of depression and the connections between neuroplasticity and visual/auditory arts, her work transforms scientific ideas into immersive experiences that encourage viewers to reflect on life's poetic nature. 她的艺术实践以在常规边界之外追寻真理为驱动，通过多媒体装置、水墨画与生成艺术探索别样的表达方式。基于对抑郁机制的研究，以及神经可塑性与视觉/听觉艺术之间的联系，她将科学理念转化为沉浸式体验，引导观者反思生命的诗意。

Limin's recent research focuses on iPSC-derived organoid development and the screening of natural compounds for neurodegenerative diseases — bridging biomedical innovation with artistic inquiry. She believes that the act of creation is itself an inquiry into existence: how we are made, how we perceive, and how we might yet become. 徐莉敏近期研究聚焦于iPSC衍生的类器官开发及针对神经退行性疾病的天然化合物筛选——在生物医学创新与艺术探索之间架起桥梁。她相信，创作本身就是对存在的追问：我们如何被塑造，如何感知，又如何可能成为另一种样子。

Ink Art Meets Science 水墨与科学的碰撞

Collaborative Art Project · 2024合作艺术项目 · 2024

2024

A collaboration with Master Wang Haikun, a lineage holder of the Li Keran ink-painting tradition. The project pairs his distinctive deconstruction of natural imagery through ink brushwork with scientific images from young researchers at CUHK Shenzhen. Both artists and scientists, working from radically different tools, are united by a single act: to dismantle what is seen in order to approach what is true. 与"李可染山水画派"传人王海琨老师合作的项目。艺术家通过具象与抽象的笔墨解构自然物象，与香港中文大学（深圳）青年学者的科研图像并置——艺术与科学，看似迥异的工具与方法，却共享同一信念：以解构所见的方式，逼近真相。

Ignorance 无明

Multi-media Installation · 2023多媒体装置 · 2023

2023

Exhibited at Art Amoy 2023, one of China's leading contemporary art fairs. Materials: plaster, Kizil earth, branches, fabric, acrylic. Inspired by the opening of José Saramago's Blindness — "I think we are blind, blind but seeing, blind people who can see, but do not see." We move through the world behind masks of our own making; the installation asks what is seen, what is unseen, and whether grief or rage are properties of the world or projections of the self. 参展2023艺术厦门国际博览会。材质：石膏、克孜尔土、树枝、布料、丙烯。灵感来自若泽·萨拉马戈《失明症漫记》开篇："我们仿佛都是盲人，看得见却看不见。"作品追问：悲伤、愤怒、恐惧，究竟是世界的真相，还是心相的外显？我们究竟看见了什么，又究竟什么都看不见。

Connection Connection

Multi-media Installation · 2023多媒体装置 · 2023

2023

Shown in Chivalrous, a group exhibition at Craft Central, London. Image: Dunhuang clay-tablet painting techniques fused with cellular microscopy. Sound: field recordings from the English Lake District. How did ancient Dunhuang painters create such luminous Guanyin imagery in cold, airless caves? Only through unwavering conviction. By learning the ancient clay-tablet technique and painting the same subject across millennia, the artist reaches across time toward those painters. The work braids cell imagery — the symbol of life — with ancient devotion, asking whether life and faith are entangled. 参展英国伦敦Craft Central《行者》群展。影像：敦煌泥板画技法融合细胞显微图像；音频：英国湖区声响录音。古代敦煌画师如何在阴冷压抑的洞窟中画出如此光明的观音？唯有坚定的信念。艺术家学习古老泥板画技法，与千年前的画家共绘同一题材，让细胞影像与古老壁画交织——追问生命与信仰是否本就同源。

The Sound of Life 生命的声音

Multi-media Installation · 2022多媒体装置 · 2022

2022

Premiered at Moment by Moment, the MFA graduation exhibition of CUHK, Hong Kong Heritage Museum. Inspired by David Bowie's music: lyrics were translated into genetic sequences, then re-converted into music. The work asks what life is, how the four-letter A-T-C-G code produces infinite variation, and whether the coded breath that animates clay in so many creation myths resembles the logic of programming. Many cultures tell of a god breathing life into dust — that breath, as Daoists say, is the moment when one gives birth to ten thousand things. The piece asks what it means to be alive, and how the invisible — genes, cells — might be made visible. 首发于香港中文大学艺术硕士毕业展《瞬间漫漫》（香港文物馆）。灵感来自David Bowie的音乐：以科学逻辑将歌词转换为基因序列，再将这段编码重新生成为音乐。作品追问何谓生命——ATCG四个密码如何万变组合，既然人类能发明编程语言创造AI，那我们是否也可能是某种智慧形态的"编程产物"？许多文化都有关于"神对泥土吹气"的故事——那口气，正是道家所谓"一生万物"的赋生时刻。

The Poetry of Life 生命的诗

Generative Installation · 2021生成艺术装置 · 2021

2021

Exhibited at Atopos, Hui Gallery, Hong Kong. Cellular microscopy transformed into contemplative visual poetry — an early exploration of the aesthetic dimensions of biological processes and the symmetries hidden in living systems. 参展香港汇画廊《Atopos》群展。细胞显微影像被转化为可凝视的视觉诗篇——这是艺术家早期对生物过程美学维度与生命系统中隐藏对称性的探索。
